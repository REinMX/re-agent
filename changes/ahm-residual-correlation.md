### Added
- Add `residual_correlation.py`: tests the mean normalized residual of each KEY, in DATE order, for lag-1 autocorrelation, bias, and variance on the saved `residuals.csv` of one ensemble. For each series it reports n, the mean, z, rho1 with its Bartlett bound 1.96/sqrt(n), n_eff, and mean(r^2) against 1 with the bound 1 + 3 sqrt(2/n). It raises `HMQC-OBSERR-AUTOCORR`, `HMQC-OBSERR-BIAS`, and `HMQC-OBSERR-VARIANCE` for a series of 8 or more dates. A shorter series is reported as insufficient, and a series with zero spread as degenerate. Neither is reported as passing. With `--truth-response` on a synthetic case it also reports the noise part and the discrepancy part of the residual. Without it, it says that it cannot separate them. It reads the member grid through `coverage.py` and refuses a non-finite value, a duplicate pair, a ragged grid, an observation without a DATE, an unparsable DATE, a repeated KEY and DATE, and a missing truth row. It never changes `ERROR`, names no cause on real data, and does not compare an ERT error-model treatment with a diagonal baseline. It is checked on analytic white noise, AR(1) noise, a constant offset, an underestimated `ERROR`, and a synthetic truth with a sinusoidal model bias.
