### Added
- Add `truth_score.py` for synthetic cases with a known truth. It reads the `residuals.csv` of `misfit_report.py` for the history and, optionally, for a truth-forecast observation file. It gives the mean-response RMS and the member-mean RMS of the prior and the posterior, and the posterior-to-prior ratio, on the same observation set. With `--interval OBS` it gives the posterior p10 and p90 of one forecast observation (numerical percentiles) and whether the truth lies between them, and it leaves that observation out of the forecast RMS. With `--dist`, `--truth` and the prior and posterior runpaths it gives, per parameter in its sampling scale, the prior and posterior sample SD, the SD reduction, the bias of the posterior mean in prior SDs, and whether the truth lies in the posterior p05 to p95. It refuses an incomplete member grid, prior and posterior observation sets that differ, and a parameter without a truth value. It sets no threshold and gives no verdict. It is checked on analytic residuals and a standardized Gaussian parameter case. On the three public lab models it reproduced all 69 frozen G3, G4 and G5 numbers to a relative 1e-9.
