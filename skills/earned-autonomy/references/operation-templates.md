# Local operation templates

Keep completed records in the approved local work log, not in portable source.
These blank templates grant no access. The site's decisions and harness controls remain authoritative.

## Job contract
```markdown
Job: <one sentence of ownership>
Owns: <one result>
Inputs: <approved roots, data classes, versions, and evidence>
May: <approved reversible actions and write roots>
Must ask before: <deletes, uploads, remote transfers, and narrower project limits>
Done when: <each check, required coverage, and independent reviewer>
When unsure: Stop the affected step and ask before a consequential choice.
Limits: <runtime, turns, command retries, and measurable cost ceiling>
Mode: <Windows/manual or approved Linux/direct>
Workflow level: <0 or human-approved level>
Authority decision: <human and evidence, not an agent self-rating>
```

## Run receipt
```markdown
Routine and run ID: <local identifiers>
Source revision and environment: <versions and any uncommitted changes>
Task and input scope: <safe description>
Acceptance checks: <pass, fail, or not run for each check>
Artifact references: <approved local references, not copied confidential contents>
Elapsed time: <measured duration or unknown>
Cost: <measured provider cost or unknown>
Human interventions: <unplanned corrections and count, or unknown>
Planned confirmations: <confirmed or parked actions, separately>
Review rounds: <count or unknown>
Side effects: <observed state and unresolved effects>
Rollback evidence: <disposable test result or not tested>
Independent review: <person or fresh context, evidence, decision, or pending>
Accepted: <yes, no, or pending>
Lesson: <local reference or none>
Next step: <exact proposal, target, and scope>
```

## Weekly review
Count from receipts with a tool, not from memory: `scripts/worklog.py report`. Do not divide by zero or hide missing records.
Report runs, accepted runs, human repairs, known mean runtime, cost coverage, and repeated failures.
Do not average unknown costs as zero. A human decides keep, repair, pause, or retire.

## Compact handoff
```markdown
Objective: <checkable outcome>
Artifacts: <approved local paths and revision>
Decisions: <approved decisions only>
Constraints: <permissions, units, data boundary, and budget>
Open questions: <unknowns and conflicts>
Next step: <person or exact action that must wait>
```
